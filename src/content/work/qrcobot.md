QRCobot makes it easy to create the QR codes and barcodes you need, save favorites, and share them with other people.

iOS widgets can place favorite codes directly on the Home Screen for quick scanning. The app has been featured by iPhone-Ticker.de.

[View QRCobot on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/qrcobot-qr-code-bar-code/id1590006394)
