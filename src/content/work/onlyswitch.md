OnlySwitch simplifies everyday Mac tasks with menu bar controls for hiding desktop icons, switching appearance, hiding the notch, keeping the Mac awake, and much more.

In December 2021, OnlySwitch reached #1 on GitHub's weekly and daily Swift trending lists. It has been covered by iFun.de, Appgefahren.de, Caschys Blog, Softpedia, OSChina, and other technology publications.
