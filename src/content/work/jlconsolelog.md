JLConsoleLog helps Swift developers inspect important application information in both development and production environments.

It is designed to complement a project's diagnostic or backdoor toolkit, making hard-to-reproduce issues easier to investigate without losing the logs that matter.

[View the source on GitHub](https://github.com/jacklandrin/JLConsoleLog)
