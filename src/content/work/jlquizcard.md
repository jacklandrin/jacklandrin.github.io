JLQuizCard is a lightweight Anki-style flashcard app built with SwiftUI. Swipe gestures move between questions, while editing tools keep questions and answers easy to maintain.

Cards can also become Lock Screen wallpapers through Shortcuts, making frequent review part of the day.

[View the source on GitHub](https://github.com/jacklandrin/JLQuizCard)
